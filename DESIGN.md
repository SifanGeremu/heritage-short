# Design
## Audience
Oromo youth and diaspora, people from Dembi Dollo and Wallaga, and short-film audiences.
## Where they watch
On a phone, shared in family chats and social feeds (9:16 first).
## Intent
At the end they should feel: grief turning into quiet, unbroken pride, the sense that the singer was silenced but the song was not.
## Logline
An elderly mother in Dembi Dollo, alone with her son's guitar, plays one note and hears young voices sing his melody back to her.
## Radio Cut v1 → v2 Self-Critique
Radio cut v1 was about 45 spoken words over a low drone. Listeners outside Dembi Dollo could not tell who the son was. v2 expands the narration to about 140 Afaan Oromo words (about 165 English words) so the first hearing carries his guitar and his songs for his people, his teaching of Qubee so children could write their names, the cassette and the poem notebook, the line *Haadha dhiiraa hin boo'in* before he left for Finfinnee, and the August 1996 silencing. The picture for shot s03 now shows the guitar, the exercise notebook, and chalk in the same room. The voice is a female Gemini TTS voice, Aoede, and the bed under the voice is soft rain instead of the drone. This note is the change made before the three-person radio-cut test below.
