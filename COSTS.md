## Cloudflare Workers AI

Measured on 25 November 2026, during `make keyframes`.

The free account stops at **10,000 neurons per day**. The API said so itself, HTTP 429: "you have used up your daily free allocation of 10,000 neurons". That was after nine keyframes had already been saved. Shot s10 was not generated.

The assignment's rough guide is 50–170 images a day. The number this account actually hit is 10,000 neurons, not a count of pictures. Logged cost on every call was 0, because the run was still on the free allowance. A list-price equivalent was not measured.
